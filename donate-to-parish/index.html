Donate to Parish

OLS
Parish · Electronics City

We warmly invite you to participate in the construction of our parish church through your generous contributions. Your support, whether big or small, will help us build a sacred space that will serve as a spiritual home for generations to come.

There are several ways you can contribute:

  • General Donations: Offer your gift as a subscription, one time donation, or ongoing support toward the construction fund.
  • Sponsorship of Church Materials: You may sponsor specific items needed for construction, such as doors, windows, square feet of flooring, bricks, or other essential materials .
  • Services and Skills: Parishioners are also welcome to extend professional services or resources that may support the project.

Every contribution whether financial, material, or service makes a meaningful difference. Together, as one parish family, we can build a beautiful house of God and a center of faith for our community. Let us join hands in this noble mission, trusting that the Lord will bless all who give with a generous heart.
