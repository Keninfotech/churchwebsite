Be a Part of
Our Sacred Mission

W

e warmly invite you to participate in the construction of our parish church through your generous contributions. Your support, whether big or small, will help us build a sacred space that will serve as a spiritual home for generations to come.

WAYS YOU CONTRIBUTE

GENERAL DONATIONS

Offer your gift as a subscription, one time donation, or ongoing support toward the construction fund.

SPONSORSHIP OF CHURCH MATERIALS

You may sponsor specific items needed for construction, such as doors, windows, square feet of flooring, bricks, or other essential materials.

SERVICES AND SKILLS

Parishioners are also welcome to extend professional services or resources that may support the project.

Every contribution whether financial, material, or service makes a meaningful difference. Together, as one parish family, we can build a beautiful house of God and a center of faith for our community. Let us join hands in this noble mission, trusting that the Lord will bless all who give with a generous heart.
