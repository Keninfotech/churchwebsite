This information reflects the vibrant structure of our parish community and the leadership entrusted with guiding each ward in faith and service.In addition, we are blessed to have among us religious members who have joyfully dedicated their lives to the service of Jesus Christ and His Church. Their vocation is a great gift to our parish, and we express our heartfelt gratitude to them. Let us join together in applauding their commitment and continue to uphold them in our prayers, asking the Lord to strengthen and bless their mission.

Saint Alphonsa of the Immaculate Conception is the first woman of Indian origin to be canonized by the Catholic Church and the first saint of the Syro-Malabar Church. Born as Anna Muttathupadathu in 1910 in Kerala, she joined the Franciscan Clarist Congregation and dedicated her life to teaching and prayer. Despite lifelong illness and suffering, she was known for her deep faith, humility, and joyful acceptance of hardship. She was beatified by Pope John Paul II in 1986 and canonized by Pope Benedict XVI in 2008.
