Cherupushpa Mission League

OLS
Parish · Electronics City

Cherupushpa mission league (cml) was officially inaugurated at the east ham mass centre on the 6th july 2014 by rev. Fr. Jose anthiyamkulam, chaplain of syro malabar church, brent wood diocese, in presence of trustees mr.paul kuruvilla, mrs.josy shajan, committe members, catechism teachers, students and all the members gathered. It was also the very same day the mass centre celebrated the feast of st. Alphonsa of bharananganam where cherupushpa mission league was originally founded. During this occasion every child from the catechism classes were given the cml badges and officially became the members of the organisation. They also took a pledge to endorse the motto of cml in their life for the creation of a better community.

Cherupushpa mission league (cml) is an indigenous lay missionary organisation and also the biggest missionary organisation for laity in asia. Cml was started under the guidance and blessings of the bishop of the changanacherry arch diocese mar james kaalassery, in the year 1947 at bharananganam by the living legend of kerala church – kunjettan (mr. P.c. Abraham pallaattukunnel) and late v. Rev. Fr. Joseph malipparambil who was the founder director of cml. Cherupushpa mission league was founded to foster vocation promotion and also to help mission activities across the country by means of man, money and prayers.

Diocese-Level CML Inauguration Our Lady of Sorrows Church, Hebbagodi was proudly represented at the Diocese-level Cherupushpa Mission League (CML) Inauguration held on 29th June 2025. The event marked the official commencement of diocesan CML activities for the academic year 2025–2026. Parish Representatives: Evan, David, Abina, Abhinav, Sharath, Charlie Leadership Achievements Abhinav was elected as the President (Core Member) of Hongasandra Forane CML. Sharath Tom was elected as the Animator representing Hongasandra Forane. We express our sincere gratitude to the parents, class teachers, and all supporters for their constant encouragement, guidance, and nominations.

Implore Series – Prayer for Persecuted Christians As part of the Implore Series with the theme “Persecuted Christians”, our students actively participated by preparing and presenting a prayer video, offering prayers and solidarity for Christians facing persecution across the world. CML Annual Day & Workshop Our students enthusiastically participated in the CML Annual Day celebrations. They took part in the Rally Competition and attended the CML Workshop, which focused on nurturing faith, leadership qualities, and missionary spirit. The active involvement of our students from Our Lady of Sorrows Church, Hebbagodi in diocesan and forane-level CML activities reflects their strong faith foundation and commitment to service and leadership. We congratulate all participants and office bearers and wish them continued success in their mission
