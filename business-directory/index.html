Parish Business & Professional Directory

To strengthen community ties and support one another, we have created the Parish Business Directory. This initiative allows parishioners to easily view details of businesses, professional services, and skilled trades offered by fellow members of our parish.

For Parishioners It provides trusted, reliable options for various services and products directly within our parish family.
For Business Owners & Professionals It offers an opportunity to share your expertise, reach fellow parishioners, and grow through mutual Christian support.
