To strengthen community ties and support one another, we have created a Parish Business Directory . This initiative allows parishioners to easily view details of businesses and services offered by fellow members of our parish.

The directory serves a mutual benefit :

  • For parishioners: it provides trusted options for various services and products within the parish community.
  • For business owners: it offers an opportunity to share their work, reach more people, and grow through the support of fellow parish members.

By making use of this directory, we not only meet our daily needs but also encourage and uplift each other’s efforts, living out the true spirit of Christian fellowship and cooperation.
