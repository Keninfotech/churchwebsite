Construction Committee

OLS
Parish · Electronics City

Since the commencement of site excavation on 28th February 2024, the church construction progressed rapidly over the last 20 months. The civil structure work from basement till the church roofing completed in this period. The major civil work that remains ahead are the church towers in front faÃ§ade and the dome above the altar. The plastering and electrical fixture installations are in progress now. Teak wood procured for the windows and doors of the church are delivered to the church site. The carpentry work must be commenced shortly for the respective civil work to complete.

The finalization phase has the interior work of the church and the community hall. This work needs expert workmanship and the engagement of experienced agencies. Lumpsum payment is a requirement for such work contracts and to progress the work as scheduled.
