Mathruvedi

OLS
Parish · Electronics City
” Being a mother is a very important role that the Lord chooses to give to many women “

OUR MOTTO

Mother, the Protector of Life and the Light of Family

Our Heavenly Patroness

Holy Virgin Mother Mary

Mathruvedi is an association which maintains a strong communion of mothers in our parish. Mathruvedi primarily focuses on enabling the mothers in the formulation of genuine families based on the Christian values. It strives to nurture and enhances the innate wealth of talents and potential within each mother. In this modern and progressive society, it ensures the participation of mothers in establishing and strengthening the Kingdom of Jesus Christ. MathruJyothi was started in the year 2010 in our Parish. Mathruvedi members have contributed immensely to all church activities and programmes.

Members actively participate in the Liturgical activities during the Holy Qurbana. Prayer meetings are conducted every month. We organize seminars for the mothers of our parish, wherein topics relevant to Parenting, Marital spirituality, spiritual empowerment of mothers, stress management, etc are covered. Members extend their support to other ministries whenever they organize activities. Annual picnics and get-togethers are organized, which promotes more bonding among members and offer a relaxed atmosphere. Mathruvedi organizes various charitable activities for orphanages, old age homes, HIV rehabilitation centers, Cancer Hospitals, etc. During Feasts and Festivals Mathruvedi helps with floral arrangements and decorations and also help in organizing the procession. On important occasions we treat our parishioners with Kerala culinary delicacies and the profit generated from this is used for charity activities.

Syro Malabar Youth Movement (SMYM) – OLS Unit

Formed in 2015 by Rev. Fr. Jose Kokandathill , the Syro Malabar Youth Movement (SMYM) of Our Lady of Sorrows (OLS) unit remains an active and integral part of the church. The youth members contribute significantly through various initiatives — from cleaning the church to organizing events and community programs.

SMYM is the official youth movement of the Syro Malabar Catholic Church , representing over 1.6 million Catholic youth worldwide. It was officially inaugurated on August 30, 2014 , following years of coordination efforts led by the Laity Commission since 2011.

Working under the Syro Malabar Youth Commission , constituted in 2015 , SMYM now flourishes across 29 dioceses and 4 mission centers in India and abroad.

The movement’s mission is to nurture faith, leadership, and social responsibility among youth while upholding the traditions of the Syro Malabar Church. Its key initiatives include:

  • Family and community outreach in slum and railway areas
  • Leadership and faith formation programs
  • Social media evangelization
  • Professional development and cultural exchange programs
  • Campus initiatives against substance abuse

Through these efforts, SMYM aims at the holistic and spiritual development of youth , empowering them to serve both the Church and society .

Vincent De Paul

OLS
Parish · Electronics City

The Catholic Church venerates Saint Vincent de Paul as an intercessor of charity. Born in Paris on April 24, 1581, Vincent de Paul was ordained to the priesthood on September 25, 1600. Through his charitable works, he embraced the poor and the needy. Vincent de Paul passed eternity on September 27, 1660. In 1712, when his tomb was opened in the presence of Cardinal Noailles, his body was found not decayed, and the linen garments remained intact. On August 13, 1729, Pope Benedict XIII declared Vincent de Paul blessed, and on June 13, 1737, Pope Clement XII proclaimed him a saint. In 1883, Pope Leo XIII recognized Vincent de Paul as an intercessor for charitable work. The message from the life of Saint Vincent is that true Christian love is manifested not in words, but through actions.

The Society of Saint Vincent de Paul (SSVP), was established in 1833 in Paris, France. The 20-year-old law student Blessed Frédéric Ozanam, along with his friends, founded SSVP. The mission of the Society is to demonstrate Christian love through actions, particularly by serving the poor and neglected in a humble, personal, and respectful manner. Today, the Vincent de Paul Society has spread worldwide. Present in over 150 countries, this organization has more than 800,000 active members and over 1.5 million volunteers. In India, it is one of the largest charitable organizations within the Catholic Church.

In our parish, the Vincent de Paul Society stands as an active witness of compassion and God’s love. What the life of Saint Vincent teaches us, and what SSVP reminds us, is the same: true love for God is demonstrated through love for the poor. In our parish, the conference has been working in solidarity with the parish community for years.

The areas of service of our conference are: education support, medical support, visits to orphanages, food kit distribution, distribution of medical equipment, and so on. All financial assistance for our activities has been provided by each one of you. The conference is grateful for each of your contributions and cooperation.

We pray that God blesses you with every possible financial and other support. Every day, at the 9 PM intercessory prayer, we offer prayers for you and your intentions. We extend special thanks to our Vicar, Assistant Vicars, Trustees, Parish Council members, Sisters, and other organizations in the parish, who always energize the activities of the conference and stand with us. We continue to request your support and cooperation in our forthcoming activities. Those who wish to work with us in the conference, may kindly contact us.

Laity Commission

The Laity Commission is the ministry body that focuses on the formation, empowerment, and active participation of lay faithful in parish life and society. It helps laypeople recognize and live their baptismal vocation through faith, service, and witness.

The Commission supports collaboration between clergy and laity, encouraging lay leadership in parish ministries, catechesis, liturgy, and outreach, while nurturing a culture of shared responsibility and mission.

Formation and Faith Education

The Laity Commission offers programs and formation sessions that help lay members grow in the Church’s teachings, spiritual life, and practical faith. Participants are encouraged to live out their Christian calling in their families, workplaces, and communities.

Lay Participation in Church Life

The Commission promotes active lay involvement in parish councils, liturgical ministries, catechetical programs, and apostolic activities. It also fosters collaboration between laypeople and clergy to build a stronger parish community.

Section

Mission & Outreach

Recognizing the family as the "domestic church," the Commission supports initiatives that strengthen Christian family life, marriage preparation, and parenting formation. It also encourages lay involvement in charitable service and social outreach.

Coordination of Lay Movements

The Commission coordinates lay movements and groups such as the Vincent de Paul Society, Legion of Mary, and other parish associations, helping them align with the Church’s mission and work together in service.

Engaging Laity in Public Life

The Laity Commission encourages lay members to bring Gospel values into public life—through education, social service, media, and community leadership—and supports ethical formation for those serving in public roles.
