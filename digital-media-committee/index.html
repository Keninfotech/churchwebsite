Digital Media Committee

Donate Us
OLS
Parish · Electronics City

In this digital transformation age, having a strong online presence, particularly a website, can have a massive impact for the growth of our church and show case our services to the community. Considering this fact, the Digital & Media committee was formed on 15th of August 2021. Few talented people who are working in the IT sector and other professions volunteered to be the members of the committee in sharing their skills for the glory of God. The main objective of the committee is to digitalize the parish database, accounts, other activities and share the parish activities reports through social media.

The website can help the parishioners’ to pay the subscription and other funds online, view the mass timings, important updates, announcements of the parish, share the photos of various activities of the parish as well as the ministries. In addition, provisions are made in the website to express and share the talents of our children and youth through social media.

All rights pertaining to this website are entrusted with the committee. The website and social media core team has the right to review and edit the contents. However, the committee is not responsible for any social media posts not adhering to the rules and regulations of Government of India.

Key Responsibilities

Parish Website Management

Maintaining and developing the parish website is one of the core responsibilities of our Media and Communication Team . The parish website serves as an important digital platform that connects our faith community, provides timely information, and strengthens our collective mission of evangelization in the modern world.

The team works diligently to ensure that the website remains up-to-date, visually engaging, and spiritually enriching . This includes:

  • Publishing parish announcements , Mass schedules, and upcoming events.
  • Updating organizational pages such as parish groups, catechism, prayer units, and ministries.
  • Sharing galleries and reports from parish celebrations, charitable works, and community activities.
  • Maintaining online resources for catechism students, parishioners, and visitors to grow in faith.
  • Integrating media content such as live-streamed Masses, YouTube links, and devotional videos.
  • Ensuring accessibility and a user-friendly interface for parishioners of all ages.

Manage Parish Social Media

Managing the social media presence of the parish is an essential responsibility of the Media and Communication Team . In today’s digital world, social media serves as a powerful tool for evangelization, community building, and sharing the joy of faith beyond the church walls.

Through platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram , the team regularly posts updates, livestreams Holy Mass and parish events, shares inspirational messages, and highlights the spiritual and social activities of the parish. These efforts help parishioners stay informed, connected, and spiritually engaged, even when they are away from the parish.

The team also ensures that all content shared reflects the values and teachings of the Catholic Church , maintaining a tone of faith, respect, and positivity. From designing creative visuals to crafting meaningful captions and responding to messages, every post aims to inspire, inform, and invite others to encounter Christ in daily life.

By maintaining an active and vibrant online presence, the parish reaches not only its members but also the wider community—spreading the Good News, promoting parish initiatives, and fostering unity through digital evangelization.

Maintain Parish Database

Maintaining the Parish Database is one of the key responsibilities of the parish administration and technical team. The database serves as the central record system of the parish, containing essential information about parishioners, families, sacraments, organizations, and community activities.

The primary goal of this responsibility is to ensure that all parish records are accurate, secure, and up to date , supporting effective communication, planning, and pastoral care. The database helps the parish team coordinate activities such as sacramental preparation, family visits, catechism registration, and event organization with greater efficiency.

Key functions include:

  • Recording and updating parishioner and family details .
  • Managing baptism, communion, confirmation, and marriage records .
  • Maintaining membership lists of parish groups and organizations.
  • Ensuring data privacy and confidentiality in compliance with Church and civil guidelines.
  • Generating reports and insights to assist in parish planning and outreach.

By maintaining a well-structured and secure database, the parish ensures smooth communication among members, better coordination of ministries, and improved pastoral support for the faithful. This effort strengthens the sense of belonging and helps the parish serve every family more personally and effectively.
