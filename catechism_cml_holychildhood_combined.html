LOOKING BACK TO THE BEGINNINGS…

Syro Malabar Kurbana was introduced in our parish in the year 2002 and back then the Catechism classes for our children were conducted alongside the Latin rites in our home parish (SFS Church). The basic idea of beginning the Catechism classes for our children was to prepare them well for their first Holy Communion.

In the year 2007, Catechism classes were completely shifted to Syro-Malabar rites and children began taking classes regularly every Sunday’s after the Holy Mass. The Catechism classes were restructured class-wise with 40 students in the year 2009-2010 .

During the year 2012-2013 our Catechism classes came under Mandya Diocese then known as Syro-Malabar Bangalore Mission. During this period the children from our Catechism classes took part in all the activities conducted in the Diocese and showcased their mantle, which made our parish well known among other parishes and the people of our diocese. In the following years our parish also became a part of the Mission League of our dioceses and actively took part in the mission League activities.

We remember in our prayers all the priests, sisters and all other members who worked hard and made their immense contribution towards bringing the Catechism classes of our parish to an “A category” level in the diocese of Mandya today, which includes MSFS brothers, La Salette brothers, DSFS sisters, SD sisters, Holy Spirit Sisters and all other Catechism Teachers. Our biggest Achievement in this period is is that two of our children, Bro. Asher Mathew and Sr. Jincy George have chosen the path of God to become the faithful missionaries of God.

Ministry

Cherupushpa Mission League

Fostering missionary spirit, prayer life, and vocation awareness among children following the patroness St. Thérèse of Lisieux.

Cherupushpa mission league (cml) was officially inaugurated at the east ham mass centre on the 6th july 2014 by rev. Fr. Jose anthiyamkulam, chaplain of syro malabar church, brent wood diocese, in presence of trustees mr.paul kuruvilla, mrs.josy shajan, committe members, catechism teachers, students and all the members gathered. It was also the very same day the mass centre celebrated the feast of st. Alphonsa of bharananganam where cherupushpa mission league was originally founded. During this occasion every child from the catechism classes were given the cml badges and officially became the members of the organisation. They also took a pledge to endorse the motto of cml in their life for the creation of a better community.

Cherupushpa mission league (cml) is an indigenous lay missionary organisation and also the biggest missionary organisation for laity in asia. Cml was started under the guidance and blessings of the bishop of the changanacherry arch diocese mar james kaalassery, in the year 1947 at bharananganam by the living legend of kerala church – kunjettan (mr. P.c. Abraham pallaattukunnel) and late v. Rev. Fr. Joseph malipparambil who was the founder director of cml. Cherupushpa mission league was founded to foster vocation promotion and also to help mission activities across the country by means of man, money and prayers.

CML Activity Report

Diocese-Level CML Inauguration

Our Lady of Sorrows Church, Hebbagodi was proudly represented at the Diocese-level Cherupushpa Mission League (CML) Inauguration held on 29th June 2025. The event marked the official commencement of diocesan CML activities for the academic year 2025–2026.

Parish Representatives: Evan, David, Abina, Abhinav, Sharath, Charlie

Leadership Achievements

Abhinav was elected as the President (Core Member) of Hongasandra Forane CML. Sharath Tom was elected as the Animator representing Hongasandra Forane.

We express our sincere gratitude to the parents, class teachers, and all supporters for their constant encouragement, guidance, and nominations.

Implore Series – Prayer for Persecuted Christians

As part of the Implore Series with the theme “Persecuted Christians”, our students actively participated by preparing and presenting a prayer video, offering prayers and solidarity for Christians facing persecution across the world.

CML Annual Day & Workshop

Our students enthusiastically participated in the CML Annual Day celebrations. They took part in the Rally Competition and attended the CML Workshop, which focused on nurturing faith, leadership qualities, and missionary spirit. The active involvement of our students from Our Lady of Sorrows Church, Hebbagodi in diocesan and forane-level CML activities reflects their strong faith foundation and commitment to service and leadership. We congratulate all participants and office bearers and wish them continued success in their mission.
