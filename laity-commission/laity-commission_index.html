Laity Commission

The Laity Commission is the ministry body that focuses on the formation, empowerment, and active participation of lay faithful in parish life and society. It helps laypeople recognize and live their baptismal vocation through faith, service, and witness.

The Commission supports collaboration between clergy and laity, encouraging lay leadership in parish ministries, catechesis, liturgy, and outreach, while nurturing a culture of shared responsibility and mission.

Formation and Faith Education

The Laity Commission offers programs and formation sessions that help lay members grow in the Church’s teachings, spiritual life, and practical faith. Participants are encouraged to live out their Christian calling in their families, workplaces, and communities.

Lay Participation in Church Life

The Commission promotes active lay involvement in parish councils, liturgical ministries, catechetical programs, and apostolic activities. It also fosters collaboration between laypeople and clergy to build a stronger parish community.

Section

Mission & Outreach

Recognizing the family as the "domestic church," the Commission supports initiatives that strengthen Christian family life, marriage preparation, and parenting formation. It also encourages lay involvement in charitable service and social outreach.

Coordination of Lay Movements

The Commission coordinates lay movements and groups such as the Vincent de Paul Society, Legion of Mary, and other parish associations, helping them align with the Church’s mission and work together in service.

Engaging Laity in Public Life

The Laity Commission encourages lay members to bring Gospel values into public life—through education, social service, media, and community leadership—and supports ethical formation for those serving in public roles.
